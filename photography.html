Through the Lens

Landscape · Portrait · Collaboration

I'm Hao Sun (孙浩), an AI researcher by profession and a passionate photographer at heart. Beyond my work in AI from Zhejiang University, I find joy in capturing the world through my lens — quiet mornings on distant mountains, fleeting expressions, the play of light across an ordinary scene.

My work focuses on landscape and portrait. From sweeping vistas to intimate human moments, I strive to document beauty wherever it appears — patient with the land, attentive with people.
